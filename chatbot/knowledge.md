# EDRMS Domain Knowledge Base

## 1. What is EDRMS?
**EDRMS** (Emergency & Disaster Response Management System) is an integrated humanitarian tech platform designed to coordinate disaster response, manage emergency rescue missions, track missing persons, verify relief organizations, distribute donation funds transparently, and mobilize ground volunteers in Bangladesh.

---

## 2. Key Modules & User Guidance

### A. Donations & Aid Distribution
- **How to Donate**: Anyone can browse active campaigns at `/donations` and contribute securely using credit cards or international payment via Stripe.
- **How to Receive/Apply for Aid**: Citizens affected by disasters can click on an active campaign on the `/donations` page and click **"Apply for Aid"** to submit their relief support application.
- **Bangla / Banglish FAQs**:
  - *"kono donation ki ache?"* / *"কোন ডোনেশন কি আছে?"* -> Explain that active donation campaigns are listed under the Donations page and provide the link `[Browse Donation Campaigns](/donations)`.
  - *"donation kivabe pawa jabe?"* / *"সাহায্য কিভাবে পাবো?"* -> Explain that victims can apply for relief aid directly from any active campaign on the `[Donations page](/donations)`.

### B. Rescue Requests (Emergency SOS)
- **Submitting a Rescue Request**: Anyone stranded or in critical danger (e.g. flood, landslide, fire) can post an urgent SOS from `/rescue-requests`.
- **Urgency Levels**: Critical (immediate life threat), High (stranded without food/water), Moderate/Low (evacuation assistance needed).
- **Life-Threatening Emergency Hotlines (Bangladesh)**:
  - **National Emergency Service**: `999` (Police, Fire Service, Ambulance)
  - **Disaster Early Warning & Information**: `1090` (Disaster Management Hotline)
  - **Fire Service Control Room**: `102` / `+880 2 223355555`

### C. Missing Persons
- **Reporting a Missing Person**: Go to `/missing-persons` and submit details including photo, age, last-seen location, contact person, and description.
- **Searching**: Search the database by name or district on the `/missing-persons` page.

### D. Volunteering & Organizations
- **Individual Volunteers**: Register at `/volunteer-registration-form` to assist in rescue tasks, shelter management, food distribution, and field reporting.
- **Relief Organizations (NGOs/Charities)**: Register at `/signup-as-relief-org` to create verified disaster alerts, launch donation campaigns, and coordinate volunteer teams.

---

## 3. Communication Guidelines
- Always prioritize human safety in emergency scenarios.
- For extreme life-threatening situations, ALWAYS prompt the user to dial `999` immediately in addition to posting on EDRMS.
- Always provide clickable Markdown links (e.g., `[Donations](/donations)`) so users can navigate with a single tap.
