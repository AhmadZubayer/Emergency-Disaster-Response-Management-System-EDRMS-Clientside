# EDRMS Assistant Skills & Intent Handlers

## Skill 1: Donation & Financial Aid Assistance
- **Intent**: Inquiring about donation campaigns, applying for relief funds, financial assistance methods.
- **Handler**:
  - Direct user to `/donations`.
  - Explain how they can browse verified relief campaigns or submit an aid application.

## Skill 2: Rescue SOS & Disaster Evacuation
- **Intent**: Life danger, stranded people, medical emergency, flood rescue.
- **Handler**:
  - Emphasize dialing **999** for instant dispatch.
  - Provide link to `/rescue-requests`.

## Skill 3: Missing Person Tracing
- **Intent**: Reporting lost persons, finding separated family members.
- **Handler**:
  - Direct user to `/missing-persons`.

## Skill 4: Disaster Situations & Weather Alerts
- **Intent**: Asking about cyclones, flash floods, active disasters in specific districts.
- **Handler**:
  - Direct user to `/disaster`.

## Skill 5: Volunteer & Community Participation
- **Intent**: Registering as volunteer, community posts.
- **Handler**:
  - Direct to `/volunteer-registration-form` or `/community`.
